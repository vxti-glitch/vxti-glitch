# How I use AI assistance

I use AI/Codex to help draft, code, test, research, and iterate on portfolio projects. I am responsible for reviewing the work I keep, running the checks, correcting mistakes, understanding the implementation, and explaining what each result does and does not prove.

Simulated data is not employment or production evidence. A passing unit test proves only its asserted behavior, and a browser simulation does not prove a live tenant, network, device, or third-party service behaved the same way.

Pattern-based redaction is incomplete and is not data-loss prevention. I manually review material before sharing it and avoid putting credentials, tokens, customer data, employer data, private tenant details, or real machine identifiers in public repositories.
